## 2. Forecasting

### Setup

* **Horizons:** h = 1, 3 and 6 h.
* **Features:** z(t) has 78 features. They are built from the last 24 h of the five indicators,
  the current Q_c and I, calendar features, and a season flag. Stage 06 builds them on the full hourly series.
* **Targets:** I(t+h) by regression; class(I(t+h)) by classification; and five indicators x(t+h).
* **Approaches:**
  * (a) forecast I directly. `a` is the regressor, `a_classifier` predicts the class directly, and
    `a_from_reg` takes the class of the regressor's I.
  * (b) forecast the five indicators, then compute I and its class with formulas (1)–(7).
* **Methods:** linear (Ridge, or logistic regression for classes), decision tree (DT), random forest (RF),
  extra trees (ET), histogram gradient boosting (HGB), kNN and MLP.
* **Baselines:** persistence (I(t+h) = I(t)) and the same hour of the previous day (I(t+h−24)).
* **S1, time split:** for each home, training uses t + h before 75 % of that home's time span.
  The test period starts at the cut-off + 24 h + h.
* **S2, homes not seen in training:** 5 groups of homes (`groups.json`), one test group at a time.
  The test hours are the late periods of the test homes, the same hours as in S1, so S1 and S2 share
  one test set for each h.
* **Tuning:** grid search on inner splits by MAE (regression) or macro-F1 (classification).
  S1 uses `TimeSeriesSplit(3)`. S2 uses 3 inner home groups with the same time cut-off rule.
* **Data used:** 40 % of training rows and 30 % of those for tuning (see Deviations).
* **Test set sizes:** about 75,000 test hours per h.
  The CRITICAL class has 7,615–7,678 test hours, so the "< 30" rule never applies.
  Class counts are in `T7c_class_counts_train_test.csv`.

### Approach (a) vs approach (b)

Over 42 (scheme, h, method) pairs, approach (b) has a lower pooled MAE than (a) in **41/42** pairs and a lower home-median MAE in 37/42. The per-home Wilcoxon difference is significant after Holm correction in 32/42 pairs. Pooled RMSE is lower for (a) in all 42 pairs: (b) has fewer typical errors but more large ones. For classes, (b) has a higher macro-F1 than the direct classifier in 32/42 pairs and a higher CRITICAL recall in 31/42. The lowest MAE in every (scheme, h) comes from approach (b). The best macro-F1 comes from (b) in 4 of 6 cases and from the direct RF classifier in the other 2 (S1 h = 6, S2 h = 3).

## 5. Deviations from the task text

1. **40 % of training rows, tuning on 30 % of them** (agreed with the author on 2026-10-15).
   * The full protocol was estimated at about 16 days on the Pi 5, mostly because of RF regression with
     max_features = 1.0 (about 1150 s per 100 trees on 226k rows).
   * The rows are drawn per home with seed 42, after the features are built. Every method of one
     (scheme, fold, h) uses the same rows. Test sets are not subsampled.
   * All model configurations and grids are as specified.
   * `T_subsample_check.csv` shows a change in MAE of ≤ 0.13 % compared with 100 % of the rows.
2. The temperature is read from `/sys/class/thermal/thermal_zone0/temp`. `vcgencmd` needs root access to
   `/dev/vcio`.
3. The Pi 5 soft-throttles at about 85 °C. The CPU ran at 85–88 °C, but no sampled frequency was below 2400 MHz.
4. The test set contains only the samples where I(t+h−24) is available, so both naive baselines are scored on
   the same hours as the models.
5. The season of a sample is the season at time t.
6. [18] uses its own configuration (the kitchen profile; season months October–March and April–September).
   Its general_residential variant is defined only for the warm period.
7. HGB and MLP classifiers use balanced sample weights, because they have no `class_weight` option.
8. RF and ET predictions from a rerun match only to 1e-12, because the order in which threads sum the trees varies.
9. The time column header is "Time Point", without the format suffix.
10. `hourly_index.parquet` is not committed because it contains raw data.
11. The S2 training class counts in `T7c` are summed over the 5 folds.
12. The example week uses the S1 test home with the highest standard deviation of I. Forecasts are plotted at time t+h.
13. Trained models (9.2 GB, `data/index_forecast/models/`, S1 only) and test predictions are not committed.
